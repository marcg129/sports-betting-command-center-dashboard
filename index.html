Metric guide: what Proj K, Model %, Market Fair %, Edge pp and EV % mean
Proj KOur model’s expected strikeouts for the pitcher.
Model %Our estimated probability that the listed Over or Under wins.
Market Fair %Pinnacle’s two-way implied probability after removing vig.
Edge (pp)Model % minus Market Fair %, measured in percentage points.
EV %Theoretical expected return at the listed price if the model probability is correct.
